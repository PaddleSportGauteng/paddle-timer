Every finalised race that's still going shows here at once — no switching. All always has everything, heats included — nothing here is ever only reachable from another tab. Semis and Finals are just a focused view for events that actually go through that progression — a straight final (small enough field to skip heats/semis entirely) stays on All only, since there's no round it's "advancing" from. A semi/final only appears here once its predecessor round's weigh-ins (podium + 1 random check) are done — it's still being computed behind the scenes, just held back from view until then.
